Commercial Plumbing in Spring, TX

Spring Express Plumbing handles commercial plumbing for businesses throughout Spring, Texas, from a single-location restaurant to multi-tenant office and retail properties. Commercial work carries its own code requirements and often a lower tolerance for downtime than residential jobs.

Grease trap maintenance is one of our most frequent commercial calls, particularly for restaurants and food service. A neglected grease trap doesn't just risk a clog, most municipal and utility agreements require regular pumping and can flag a property for non-compliance if records aren't kept current.

We also see a meaningful amount of commercial leak detection work, often for property managers responsible for buildings they don't occupy day to day, where a leak can run for a while before anyone on site notices.

Retail and office properties bring a different set of common calls, restroom fixture repair and replacement, water heater service for smaller tenant spaces, and code-driven upgrades tied to renovations or a change in occupancy type. Each of these carries its own compliance considerations that a straightforward residential repair doesn't.

2

How We Work With Commercial Properties

For property managers and multi-tenant sites, we coordinate scheduling around business hours or after-hours windows depending on what minimizes disruption to tenants or customers, and we can work directly with a property management company rather than requiring an on-site decision-maker for every visit.

For restaurants specifically, we understand that a plumbing issue during service hours is a genuine emergency for the business, not just an inconvenience, and we prioritize accordingly.

All fixture and compliance work follows the IPC and, where applicable, local accessibility requirements for commercial restrooms and fixture counts.

We also keep documentation for commercial clients, service records, grease trap pumping logs, and backflow test results, organized and available on request, since commercial properties often need that paperwork for their own compliance records or lease requirements.

Common Questions

Do you offer after-hours service for commercial properties?
Yes. We understand a plumbing issue during business hours can mean real lost revenue, especially for restaurants, and we prioritize commercial emergency calls accordingly.
How often does a grease trap need to be pumped?
It depends on the size of the trap and your volume of food service, but most local requirements call for regular pumping on a set schedule, sometimes monthly. We can review your specific agreement and set up a standing schedule.
Can you work directly with our property management company instead of on-site staff?
Yes, we regularly coordinate directly with property managers for multi-tenant buildings, including scheduling, billing, and reporting back on completed work.
Do you keep service records for our commercial property?
Yes. Grease trap logs, backflow test results, and general service history are kept and available on request, which many commercial clients need for their own lease or compliance paperwork.
Do you offer service agreements for multiple commercial locations?
Yes, for businesses or property groups with more than one site in the area, we can set up a standing service agreement covering all locations under consistent scheduling and pricing.
Do you work on medical or industrial facilities, not just restaurants and offices?
We handle general commercial plumbing across most property types. For specialized medical or industrial systems with unique regulatory requirements, call and describe your facility and we'll tell you honestly whether it's a good fit for our team.
