Drain Cleaning & Unclogging in Spring, TX

Spring Express Plumbing clears clogged drains across Spring, Texas around the clock, from a single slow bathroom sink to a main line backup flooding a laundry room. Emergency drain calls are answered 24/7, not routed to a morning callback.

A drain that backs up more than once in the same spot within a few weeks is usually not a one-time clog. It is a sign of a partial blockage further down the line, often root intrusion in an older section of pipe, that keeps narrowing until it closes off completely.

Old Town Spring and the older sections of Harris County closer to it were largely built out with clay or cast iron sewer laterals. Mature trees planted decades ago have root systems that have had plenty of time to find and work into aging pipe joints. Newer construction further from the historic core is less prone to this specific issue, but not immune to grease buildup or a bellied section of line.

2

How We Clear It

A standard fixture clog gets cleared with a drain snake sized to the line. If the same drain keeps backing up, we run a sewer camera down the line first to see exactly what is causing it, root intrusion, a bellied section of pipe, or grease buildup, rather than guessing and re-snaking the same spot every few months.

Grease and scale buildup that a snake cannot fully clear gets hydro jetted, which uses high-pressure water to scour the full diameter of the pipe instead of just punching a hole through the clog.

3

Snaking, Hydro Jetting, or Camera-Guided Repair

Drain snaking

Fast and effective for a single fixture clog with no history of repeat backups. Our standard first move on most calls.

Hydro jetting

The right call for grease-lined kitchen lines or a main line with a history of repeat clogs, since it clears the full pipe wall, not just a channel through the blockage.

Camera-guided spot repair

When a camera inspection finds a specific defect, a cracked joint or root intrusion at one point, a targeted repair can fix the actual cause instead of clearing the same clog again next year.

Common Questions

Do you clear drains after hours in Spring, TX?
Yes, 24/7. An active backup does not wait for morning, and a real dispatcher answers the call any hour to get a plumber routed out.
Why does my kitchen sink keep clogging?
Repeat kitchen sink clogs almost always mean grease buildup lining the pipe rather than a single blockage. A standard snake punches through it temporarily, but hydro jetting clears the full pipe wall and usually stops the repeat calls.
How do I know if it's a main line clog and not just one drain?
If more than one fixture backs up at the same time, a toilet flush causing the shower to gurgle, for example, that points to the main line rather than a single fixture. That is worth a camera inspection instead of repeated snaking.
Does pouring grease down the kitchen drain really cause clogs?
Yes, more than almost anything else we see. Grease goes down warm and liquid, then cools and hardens against the pipe wall further along the line, narrowing it a little more with each use until it closes off. Letting grease cool in a container and tossing it instead saves a real service call down the road.
