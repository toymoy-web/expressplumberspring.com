Spring Lakes at a Glance

Spring Lakes is a residential community of 786 single-family homes in Spring, Texas 77373. It sits east of I-45 between Cypresswood Drive and Louetta Road, near Old Town Spring and the Hardy Toll Road. A 2018 Community Impact profile reported that it was fully built out in 2013 and lists walking trails, playgrounds, a swimming pool, and tennis courts.

That profile lists Spring ISD among the community's taxing entities, which sets it apart from the nearby Klein ISD communities. It is from 2018, so confirm fees and details with your association.

Homes Built Out by 2013

Because Spring Lakes finished building in 2013, its homes are among the newer ones in the Spring area, roughly thirteen years old or more. Builder warranties are well behind most owners by now. Water heaters, fixtures, and shutoff valves are entering the age where routine replacement starts. That does not mean a problem exists.

A common first call is a water heater that leaks or no longer heats. Tank units often last roughly 8 to 12 years, so a home from the early 2010s may be at the point where repair versus replacement is worth discussing. See water heater repair for options.

Hard Water and Fixtures

Water hardness differs by source and district, so check your district's water quality report. White crust on faucets and spotted glassware are common signs of hard water anywhere.

Scale shortens the life of faucets, shower heads, and water heaters. Our faucet and sink repair and toilet repair pages cover repairs, and our softener installation page covers treatment.

4

After Heavy Rain

Heavy rain is a fact of life across the Houston area, and we make no claim about flood history for this community. After any storm, the plumbing steps are the same. Shut off water if a line breaks, have the water heater checked before it restarts, and look for new leaks.

Wet drywall and flooring are handled by our water damage team, which has its own page and number.

Common Questions

Where is Spring Lakes located?
It is east of I-45 between Cypresswood Drive and Louetta Road in Spring, TX 77373, near Old Town Spring and the Hardy Toll Road.
Which school district serves Spring Lakes?
A 2018 profile lists Spring ISD among its taxing entities. Plumbing service is the same either way, and the school district can confirm zoning for your address.
My home is newer. Is it too soon for water heater problems?
Not always. Tank units often last roughly 8 to 12 years, so homes finished around 2013 may be reaching that range. We check the unit before suggesting any repair.
Is my builder warranty still active?
Builder warranties usually expire well before a home reaches this age. If a manufacturer warranty still applies to a part, keep the paperwork handy and tell us when you call.
How can I tell if my water is hard?
White crust on faucets and spotted glassware are common signs. Your water district's quality report lists hardness. We can discuss softener options if scale is a problem.
Do you cover Spring Lakes the same as Old Town Spring?
Yes. Both are served from one dispatch line with the same approach to pricing. Read your street address to the dispatcher and we will confirm coverage.
