Tankless Water Heater Service in Spring, TX

Spring Express Plumbing installs, flushes, and repairs tankless water heaters throughout Spring, Texas. A tankless unit heats water on demand instead of storing it, which means no tank to run out and, done right, a real reduction in standby energy loss compared to an older tank unit.

Most of the tankless calls we get do not start as a request for a new unit. They start as a complaint about something else, lukewarm water partway through a shower, a unit that takes longer than it used to reach temperature, or a strange clicking sound on startup. Those are usually scale buildup or a flow sensor issue, not a sign the unit is dead.

2

Lukewarm Water or a Unit That Takes Longer to Heat Up?

If your tankless unit used to deliver hot water almost instantly and now takes ten or fifteen seconds longer, or the temperature dips when a second fixture turns on, that is usually mineral scale building up on the heat exchanger, not a failing unit. Houston-area municipal water carries enough dissolved mineral content that an un-flushed tankless system will scale up over a few years. A descale service run through the isolation valves, if your installer put them in, often restores full performance without replacing anything.

3

How We Approach a Tankless Call

We start by checking whether the unit has isolation valves for flushing. Many builder-installed units in newer Spring subdivisions do, and a straightforward descale with a pump and a mild acid solution clears the scale in under an hour.

If there are no isolation valves, or the unit is old enough that a flush will not fix the underlying issue, we check the flow sensor, the igniter, and the venting before recommending anything. Gas-fired tankless units depend on correct venting, and a partially blocked vent will throw error codes that look like a heating problem.

Gas venting on every install follows the National Fuel Gas Code (ANSI Z223.1) and the IPC alongside the manufacturer's own venting specifications, not a generalized assumption carried over from a tank unit's setup.

4

Repair, Descale, or Replace

Descale service

The right call for a unit under 8 to 10 years old showing slow heat-up or temperature swings, with no error codes pointing to a hard failure.

Component repair

Flow sensors, igniters, and heat exchangers are individually replaceable on most units. Worth it when the unit is mid-life and the rest of the system is sound.

Replacement

Makes sense on a unit past 12-15 years, or when repair costs start approaching what a new unit installed would run. We will tell you honestly which side of that line you are on.

Common Questions

Do tankless water heaters actually save money in Spring, TX?
Over the life of the unit, yes, mainly by cutting standby heat loss since there is no tank sitting hot all day. The upfront cost is higher than a tank unit, and gas conversions can need line or venting work, so the payback period depends on your current setup.
How often should a tankless unit be flushed?
About once a year for most Spring homes on municipal water. Homes with a water softener already installed can often stretch that interval, since softened water scales the heat exchanger more slowly.
Can you convert my tank water heater to tankless?
In most cases yes. We check your existing gas line size and venting first, since a straight swap sometimes needs both upgraded to run a tankless unit safely.
