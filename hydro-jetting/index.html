Hydro Jetting in Spring, TX

Spring Express Plumbing runs hydro jetting service for homes and businesses across Spring, Texas whose drain lines keep clogging faster than a snake can reasonably keep up with. Jetting uses pressurized water through a specialized nozzle to scour grease, scale, and root intrusion off the full interior of the pipe.

This is a different tool for a different problem than routine drain cleaning. A snake punches a channel through a blockage and moves on. Jetting removes the buildup lining the pipe wall that caused the blockage in the first place, which is why it works on lines that keep clogging in the same spot every few months.

2

Snaked the Same Drain More Than Once This Year?

If a line has been snaked two or three times for the same recurring clog, the underlying buildup on the pipe wall is still there between visits, and it will keep narrowing the line until it closes again. Grease-lined kitchen lines, older cast iron with root intrusion near Old Town Spring, and commercial kitchen lines with a grease trap all show this pattern. Jetting breaks that cycle instead of managing it every few months.

3

How the Service Works

We run a sewer camera first when the history is not already clear, so we know what we are dealing with, grease, roots, scale, or a structural defect, before applying pressure that could push debris into a compromised section of pipe.

The jetting hose runs the length of the line with a nozzle sized to the pipe diameter, using water pressure to strip the interior wall clean rather than just breaking through the center of the clog.

After jetting, we run the camera again on request so you can actually see the difference rather than taking our word for it. A pipe that was visibly lined with grease or scale before the service looks like new pipe afterward when it's gone well.

Common Questions

Is hydro jetting safe for older pipes?
For sound pipe, yes. For pipe with existing structural damage, root intrusion has already cracked a joint, for example, high pressure can worsen the problem, which is why we camera-inspect first when the pipe's condition isn't already known.
How is hydro jetting different from snaking?
A snake clears a path through a clog. Jetting scours the entire interior wall of the pipe clean, which is why it stops the same clog from coming back every few months the way repeated snaking often doesn't.
Do you offer hydro jetting for commercial kitchens?
Yes, including grease trap lines that need more frequent service than a typical residential kitchen line due to higher grease volume.
Will jetting damage my pipes?
Sound pipe handles jetting pressure without issue. That's exactly why we camera-inspect first when a line's condition isn't already documented, so we're not applying pressure to a section that's already structurally compromised.
How often should a grease-prone kitchen line be jetted?
For a busy home kitchen, roughly once a year keeps buildup from reaching problem levels. Commercial kitchens with heavier grease volume often need it quarterly, which we can set up as a standing schedule.
