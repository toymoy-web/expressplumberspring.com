Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 2026

Spring Express Plumbing ("we," "us," "our") respects your privacy. This policy explains what information we collect when you visit expressplumberspring.com or call our dispatch line, and how we use it.

Information We Collect

When you call us, we collect the phone number you call from, the information you provide about your plumbing issue, and your service address. When you browse our website, standard technical information (browser type, pages visited, general location) may be collected through normal web server logs.

How We Use Information

We use the information you provide to dispatch a plumber, schedule service, and follow up on completed work. We do not sell your personal information to third parties. Call recordings, where used, are for quality and training purposes.

Third-Party Services

Our website may use third-party services for call tracking, analytics, and mapping. These services may collect information according to their own privacy policies.

Your Choices

You can ask us at any time what information we have on file for your address or phone number by contacting us directly.

Contact Us

Questions about this policy can be directed to contact@expressplumberspring.com or (281) 626-9205.
