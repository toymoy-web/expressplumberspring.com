Sewer Line Repair in Spring, TX

Spring Express Plumbing repairs sewer lateral lines across Spring, Texas, from a single cracked joint letting roots in to a fully collapsed section needing excavation. This is structural repair to the line itself, distinct from clearing a clog that a snake or jetting can handle on its own.

Old Town Spring and nearby older sections of Harris County were largely built out with clay or cast iron sewer laterals, materials that develop joint separations and root intrusion points as they age. Newer PVC lines in further-out subdivisions fail less often from roots but are not immune to a bellied section from ground settling.

2

How We Diagnose It

A sewer camera goes down the line first, always. That tells us exactly where the defect is, whether it's a single joint, a longer bellied section, or a fully collapsed run, before we recommend a repair method rather than guessing based on symptoms alone.

Once we know the defect's location and extent, we can usually tell you on the spot whether a spot repair or a longer trenchless solution makes more sense for that specific section.

We keep the camera footage on file for the job, so if a question comes up later about what was found or what was repaired, there's an actual record rather than relying on memory of the visit.

Every repair method we use meets the IPC as adopted and amended by the Texas State Board of Plumbing Examiners, whether that's a spot repair, trenchless lining, or full excavation.

3

Spot Repair, Trenchless Lining, or Excavation

Spot repair

For a single defect location, digging just that section is often faster and less disruptive than a full-line solution.

Trenchless pipe lining

A resin liner cured inside the existing pipe, avoiding a full excavation for lines with multiple smaller defects along their length.

Excavation and replacement

Necessary when a section has fully collapsed or degraded beyond what lining can reliably fix.

✓

What to Do Before We Arrive

Note when the backup or slow drain started and whether it's happened before in the same spot.
Avoid running water or flushing toilets once a backup is active, to keep it from worsening before we get there.
Clear vehicles and outdoor items away from the likely line path in the front or side yard if you know roughly where it runs.
Have any prior camera inspection or repair records handy if this issue has come up before.

Common Questions

How do I know if I need sewer line repair instead of just a cleanout?
Recurring backups in the same spot, especially after the line has been snaked or jetted more than once for the same issue, point to a structural problem rather than a simple clog. A camera inspection settles it either way.
Can tree roots really break into a sewer line?
Yes, and it's one of the most common issues we find on older Spring properties. Roots find a joint separation or hairline crack and grow into it seeking moisture, gradually widening the opening and worsening the blockage over time.
Do you offer trenchless sewer repair in Spring, TX?
Yes, for lines where the defects are limited enough that lining makes sense. We'll tell you honestly if your line's condition calls for excavation instead.
How long does sewer line repair take?
A spot repair is often completed in a single day. Trenchless lining typically takes a day for the lining itself plus curing time. A full excavation and replacement runs longer, and we'll give you a real timeline once we know the actual scope.
