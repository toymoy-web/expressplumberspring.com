Klein Is a Community, Not a City

Spring Express Plumbing dispatches to Klein, Texas around the clock. Klein is an unincorporated community in north Harris County with no city government of its own. It sits inside Houston's extraterritorial jurisdiction and is bounded roughly by SH 99 to the north, SH 249 to the west, I-45 to the east, and the city of Houston to the south.

Klein overlaps the area most people call Spring, so a Klein address often carries a Spring mailing label. If your bill says Spring, TX 77379, 77388, or 77389, you may well be in Klein. We run both from one dispatch line, and we keep Klein on its own page because its history and school-district identity are distinct.

Klein ISD Zones Instead of City Limits

Klein has no city limits, so the practical way to place an address is by school zone, creek, and the roads around it. The Klein Independent School District covers about 88 square miles and enrolled more than 52,000 students in the 2020 to 2021 school year, which hints at how many homes sit inside the boundary.

The community's roots go back to German immigrants who farmed along Cypress Creek in the 1840s. Early Klein ISD schools carry the surnames of those settlers, including Klein, Wunderlich, Theiss, and Lemm. Today the area mixes homes built across many decades with newer neighborhoods served by Municipal Utility Districts, so the plumbing conversation starts with when the house was built.

Creeks, Storms, and Klein Plumbing Calls

Klein sits among creeks that feed Spring Creek, and the area has a documented flood history. Heavy rain in April 2016 and Hurricane Harvey in August 2017 closed Spring and Klein schools for a week or more. After storms like that, plumbing calls often involve broken lines, dead sump pumps, and water heaters that need a safety check before they restart.

Most Klein homes sit on slabs, so a sump pump is not standard equipment. It does matter for crawlspaces, yard drainage pits, and below-grade areas. We check where the water is actually coming from before recommending one.

Common Questions

Do you cover every ZIP code in Klein?
Yes. We dispatch to the Klein ZIP codes 77379, 77388, and 77389 under the same pricing as the rest of our area, from the Spring Cypress Road corridor out to SH 249 and SH 99. The Klein name also reaches the Houston-labeled ZIP codes 77066, 77069, and 77086 along the southern edge, so call and we will confirm your address.
Is your emergency line staffed for Klein calls at night?
Yes. A real dispatcher answers at any hour and routes a plumber to Klein. After-hours trips may carry a trip fee, and we state it before any work begins.
Is Klein the same as Spring for service purposes?
For dispatch, yes. Klein overlaps the area most people call Spring, and both run from one phone number and one pricing approach. You do not need to know which label your address uses.
Which water utility serves my Klein home?
It depends on the address. Klein is unincorporated, so water usually comes from a Municipal Utility District instead of a city. Your water bill names the district, and it matters for the hardness and pressure questions we ask on the phone.
Do Klein homes need a sump pump?
Most do not, because typical Houston-area homes sit on slabs with no basement. A pump makes sense for a crawlspace, a drainage pit, or a below-grade space that collects water. We look at where the water is coming from before recommending one.
Can you help with plumbing problems after a storm or flood?
Yes, with the plumbing side. We shut off water, repair broken lines, and check water heaters before they restart. Soaked drywall and flooring fall under our water damage restoration team, which has its own number on that page.
What if my mailing address says Spring but I think I am in Klein?
Call and read us the street address. We will confirm coverage and route a plumber either way, since Klein and Spring share one dispatch line.
