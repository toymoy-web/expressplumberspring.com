Water Heater Installation & Replacement in Spring, TX

Spring Express Plumbing installs and replaces both tank and tankless water heaters across Spring, Texas, including the older galvanized-plumbing homes near Old Town Spring and the newer construction further out toward the Montgomery County line.

Most tank water heaters last somewhere between 8 and 12 years in this area. The clay-heavy Gulf Coast soil does not directly shorten a tank's life the way it does buried pipe. But the mineral content in North Harris County Regional Water Authority supply water does add sediment over time, and that is the more common failure driver we see locally.

Texas plumbing code requires a thermal expansion tank on a closed water system, meaning any home with a backflow preventer or check valve at the meter. It is one of the most common things missing on an older installation, and we check for it on every replacement rather than assuming the last plumber caught it.

2

What a Same-Day Replacement Actually Involves

We inspect the existing supply lines, the shutoff valve, the venting or electrical connection, and the drain pan before ordering a unit, so the quote reflects the real job, not just the appliance cost.

Most standard tank swaps in a garage or closet location are completed same visit. Attic installs and any job that needs a new expansion tank, updated venting, or a code-required drain pan take longer, and we will tell you that before starting, not after.

A permit is typically required for a water heater replacement in unincorporated Harris and Montgomery County, and we pull it as part of the job rather than skipping the inspection to save time. Skipping the permit step is also one of the more common reasons a homeowner's insurance claim gets denied later if the unit ever fails and causes damage.

3

Tank, High-Efficiency Tank, or Tankless

Standard tank replacement

The straightforward choice when the old unit was a tank and the household usage has not changed. Lowest upfront cost, most familiar maintenance.

High-efficiency tank

Worth considering if your gas or electric bill has crept up, since these units insulate and recover heat better than a builder-grade tank.

Switch to tankless

Makes sense for households that regularly run out of hot water or want the standby energy savings, with the trade-off of a higher installed cost.

Common Questions

How long does a water heater replacement take?
Most standard tank swaps take two to three hours. Attic installs, tankless conversions, or jobs needing code upgrades like a new expansion tank typically run longer, and we will give you a real time estimate on site.
My water heater is leaking from the bottom. Can it be repaired?
A tank leaking from the bottom almost always means the tank itself has failed internally, which is not repairable. At that point replacement is the only real option, and we would tell you that honestly rather than charging for a repair that will not hold.
Do you install water heaters in older Old Town Spring homes?
Yes. Older homes sometimes have galvanized supply lines or undersized electrical for a modern unit, which we check before installing so the new water heater actually performs the way it should.
