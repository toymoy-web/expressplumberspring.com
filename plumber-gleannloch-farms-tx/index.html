Gleannloch Farms at a Glance

Gleannloch Farms is a large master-planned community in unincorporated Harris County. Sources describe it as roughly 2,100 acres, bounded by Spring Cypress Road to the south, Champion Forest Drive to the east, and Boudreaux Road to the north, with an irregular western edge toward SH 249. Most addresses use Spring 77379, and some parts use 77388.

The land was an Arabian horse farm in the 1950s, and homes began going up in the late 1990s. Building continued for more than a decade, so the community holds houses from many different years in gated and non-gated sections. Exact boundaries are not published in one place, so we confirm your address by phone.

MUD Water, Sections, and HOA Rules

Gleannloch Farms homes sit inside a municipal utility district that funds water and sewer service, and tax rates differ by section. Your water and tax bills name the district. A community association with a resident board and a management company also governs the area, and fees and rules differ by section.

Before exterior plumbing work such as a new hose bib, a yard drain, or a softener loop, check your section's deed restrictions. Tell the dispatcher what the job involves and we will describe it clearly so you can ask the association.

Golf Frontage and Irrigation

Gleannloch Pines Golf Club, a 27-hole course, winds through the community, and many yards have lawn irrigation. Irrigation systems use backflow preventers to keep lawn water out of the drinking supply. They are a normal part of any sprinkler hookup and need periodic testing. See our backflow testing page for details.

A broken sprinkler line can also run unseen for days and show up as a high water bill. If your bill jumps with no change in use, a leak detection visit can trace the source before you pay to dig.

Homes Built Over Many Years

Because building ran from the late 1990s into the 2000s and beyond, a Gleannloch Farms house may be fairly new or more than twenty years old. A house that age is often on its second water heater, and tank units commonly last roughly 8 to 12 years.

We check yours by condition and age rather than assuming it needs replacing. If a unit is leaking or failing, our water heater repair and replacement pages explain the options.

Common Questions

Which ZIP codes does Gleannloch Farms use?
Most of the community uses 77379, and some parts use 77388. A ZIP code is only a mailing label, so read your street address to the dispatcher and we will confirm coverage.
Do you work in the gated sections?
Yes. Tell the dispatcher which section you live in and whether we need a gate code or should meet you at the entrance.
Is Gleannloch Farms inside a city?
No. It sits in Harris County outside any city limits, which is why a municipal utility district provides water and sewer. The Spring name refers to the postal area.
Should I call you or my utility district about a high water bill?
Billing questions go to your utility district. If you suspect a leak is behind the bill, call us and we can test the lines and fixtures.
Do Gleannloch Farms homes need a sump pump?
Most Houston-area homes sit on slabs, so a sump pump is not typical. Some properties have drainage pits or below-grade areas. We check where the water comes from before suggesting equipment.
Are parts of Gleannloch Farms in a flood zone?
Some portions are mapped in flood zones and others are not, so check the flood maps for your address. On the plumbing side, we shut off water, repair broken lines, and check the water heater after a storm.
