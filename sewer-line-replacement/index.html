Sewer Line Replacement in Spring, TX

When a sewer lateral is degraded beyond what a spot repair or lining can reliably fix, a full replacement is the honest answer, and we'll tell you when it actually is.

Sewer Line Replacement in Spring, TX

Spring Express Plumbing replaces sewer laterals throughout Spring, Texas when a line has failed too broadly for the repair or trenchless lining options covered on our sewer line repair page to make sense. This is a distinct, larger scope of work from a spot repair, and we don't recommend it unless the line's actual condition calls for it.

Some older Spring properties still have sections of Orangeburg pipe, a tar-and-wood-fiber material used from roughly the 1940s through the 1970s that degrades from the inside and eventually deforms or collapses. A line in that condition is a genuine replacement case, not a repair candidate.

Replacement is always the larger, more disruptive option compared to repair or lining, which is exactly why we don't recommend it unless the camera inspection actually supports it. We'd rather line or spot-repair a line that qualifies for it than sell a bigger job than the pipe needs.

2

How We Decide Replacement Is the Right Call

A camera inspection always comes first. If the defects are limited to one or two spots, repair or lining is the better value. If the camera shows the pipe material itself failing broadly along its length, multiple collapsed sections, severe corrosion, or a deformed Orangeburg line, replacement is the honest recommendation.

We walk through the actual camera footage with you before recommending replacement, so it's not a judgment call you're asked to take on faith.

Replacement work meets the IPC as adopted and amended by the Texas State Board of Plumbing Examiners, covering pipe material, slope, and bedding requirements for the new line.

3

Trenchless or Open Trench Replacement

Trenchless pipe bursting

Pulls a new pipe through while breaking apart the old one, avoiding a full-length open trench for most of the run.

Open trench replacement

Sometimes the more direct option depending on depth, existing landscaping, and what's above the line's path.

Full permit and inspection process

Every replacement is permitted and inspected per Harris and Montgomery County requirements, not treated as optional paperwork.

✓

What to Expect on Replacement Day

Clear vehicles and outdoor items from the likely path of the line and any access pits.
Expect water to be off for part of the day while the connection is made.
Landscaping directly above a trenchless path is usually undisturbed; open trench work will need restoration afterward.
Keep pets away from any open access pits or trench areas until work is complete.

Common Questions

How do I know if I need replacement instead of repair or lining?
A camera inspection settles it. If the pipe material is failing broadly rather than at one or two isolated points, or it's Orangeburg pipe in a degraded state, replacement is the honest recommendation, and we'll show you the footage before making that call.
Will replacing my sewer line destroy my yard?
With trenchless pipe bursting, often the disruption is much less than people expect, limited mostly to small access pits rather than a trench the full length of the line. Open trench work is more disruptive but sometimes still the better option depending on conditions.
How long does a full sewer line replacement take?
Trenchless replacement is often completed in one to two days. Open trench replacement can take longer depending on length and restoration needed afterward. We'll give you a real timeline once we know the scope.
Is trenchless replacement more expensive than open trench?
Often per foot, yes, but it usually avoids the cost of restoring landscaping, driveways, or hardscaping afterward, which can close the gap or even favor trenchless once total cost is considered.
