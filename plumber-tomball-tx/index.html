Plumbing in a Fast-Growing City

Spring Express Plumbing dispatches to Tomball, Texas around the clock. Tomball is its own incorporated city, established in 1907, with its own council-manager government and water service run through several independent Municipal Utility Districts rather than the utility that serves Spring proper.

The city has grown fast. ZIP 77375 alone added roughly a quarter more residents between 2013 and 2018, and that pace has continued. That growth means we see two very different kinds of plumbing calls here: newer PEX-plumbed homes in subdivisions built over the last decade, and older properties closer to Tomball's original downtown core with plumbing that has had more time to age.
