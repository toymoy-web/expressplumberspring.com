Water Filtration Installation in Spring, TX

Spring Express Plumbing installs whole-home and point-of-use water filtration throughout Spring, Texas. North Harris County Regional Water Authority supplies a blend of groundwater and treated surface water to most of the area, and the exact taste, hardness, and mineral content can vary block to block depending on which source is dominant at a given time.

Most filtration calls do not start with someone asking for a specific system. They start with a complaint: a chlorine smell from the tap, spots on glassware and shower doors, or skin and hair that feels different after moving into the area.

2

Chlorine Taste, Hard Water Spots, or Skin Irritation?

A chlorine or pool-like smell from the tap points to chloramine or chlorine used in municipal treatment, which a carbon filtration stage removes effectively. White spots on dishes and shower glass, or soap that will not lather well, point to water hardness from dissolved calcium and magnesium, which needs a softener or a combination system rather than carbon filtration alone. Dry skin or hair after a shower can come from either issue, so we test before recommending a specific system rather than guessing.

3

How We Match a System to Your Water

We run a basic hardness and chlorine test at the point of use before recommending anything, since a softener solves a hardness problem but does nothing for taste and odor, and a carbon filter solves taste and odor but does nothing for hardness.

For households wanting both, a combination system pairs a softener with a carbon or reverse osmosis stage. For anyone who only wants better drinking and cooking water without treating the whole house, an under-sink system is the lower-cost option.

We size the system to your actual household, not a generic household-of-four assumption. A larger family or a home with more bathrooms running simultaneously needs a system rated for higher flow, or performance drops noticeably at peak usage times.

Installation follows the IPC's backflow and cross-connection requirements, which matter specifically for any system that ties into the main line rather than sitting entirely under a single sink.

4

Whole-Home, Under-Sink, or Combination

Whole-home carbon filtration

Treats water at the point it enters the house, addressing chlorine taste and odor at every fixture, not just the kitchen sink.

Under-sink reverse osmosis

The right call for drinking and cooking water specifically, without the cost of treating water used for showers and laundry.

Combination softener and filtration

Addresses both hardness and taste in one system, worth it for households dealing with both spots and odor complaints.

Common Questions

Do I need a water softener or a water filter?
They solve different problems. A softener addresses hardness (spots, poor lather). A filter addresses taste and odor from chlorine or sediment. We test your water before recommending one, the other, or both.
Is Spring, TX water hard?
It varies by which part of the North Harris County Regional Water Authority's blended supply reaches your specific block. We test at your home rather than quoting a citywide number that may not reflect your actual tap water.
How long does whole-home filtration installation take?
Most installations take three to five hours depending on where the main line enters the house and whether any additional piping is needed to route through the system.
Do filtration systems need regular maintenance?
Yes. Carbon filters need periodic cartridge changes, and softeners need salt refilled on a regular schedule. We go over the actual maintenance schedule for whatever system we install rather than leaving you to guess.
Can I install a filtration system myself?
A basic under-sink filter, sometimes. Whole-home systems tie into the main line and usually need a licensed plumber to avoid pressure or backflow issues down the line.
