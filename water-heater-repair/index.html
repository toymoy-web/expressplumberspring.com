Water Heater Repair in Spring, TX

Spring Express Plumbing repairs tank and tankless water heaters across Spring, Texas, and takes emergency no-hot-water calls 24/7. Cold showers do not wait for a convenient appointment window, and neither do we.

Most water heater repair calls fall into a handful of real patterns: no hot water at all, water that starts hot and goes cold fast, a popping or rumbling sound from the tank, rust-colored water, or a rotten egg smell from the hot tap specifically. Each points to a different, usually fixable, cause.

2

What Each Symptom Usually Means

A popping or rumbling sound is almost always sediment that has built up on the bottom of the tank, common on units that have never been flushed. A rotten egg smell limited to hot water specifically points to the anode rod reacting with the water, not a gas leak, though we check for both to be sure.

No hot water on a gas unit often traces back to a pilot light or igniter problem. On an electric unit, it is frequently one of the two heating elements failing rather than the whole unit. We test the actual component before replacing anything.

Most manufacturers cover tank water heaters for 6 to 12 years depending on the model tier, and many repairs we make are still within that window. We check your unit's manufacture date, usually stamped on a label near the top, before quoting a repair so you know whether a warranty claim might cover part of the cost.

Any component we replace, including venting or gas line work, is installed to the IPC as enforced by the Texas State Board of Plumbing Examiners, not a shortcut chosen to save a return trip.

3

Repair Options by Cause

Sediment flush

Clears popping and rumbling noise and often restores lost capacity, worth trying before assuming the tank itself is failing.

Component replacement

Heating elements, thermostats, igniters, and thermocouples are individually replaceable on most units without touching the tank itself.

Anode rod replacement

A straightforward fix for hot-water-only odor that extends the tank's remaining life instead of masking the smell.

Thermostat recalibration

Sometimes water that runs lukewarm instead of hot is simply a thermostat set or drifted too low, a quick fix that's worth ruling out before anything else.

Common Questions

Do you repair water heaters after hours in Spring, TX?
Yes, 24/7. A real dispatcher answers and routes a plumber for a genuine no-hot-water emergency any time of day or night.
Why does my hot water smell like rotten eggs?
Almost always the anode rod reacting with sulfur bacteria in the water, limited to the hot tap because it only happens inside the tank. Replacing the anode rod usually resolves it and adds life to the tank.
Is it worth repairing an older water heater or should I replace it?
Depends on the specific failure. A single part failure on a tank under 8 years old is usually worth repairing. Past 10-12 years, or with a tank that has already leaked once, we will tell you honestly when replacement is the better value.
How quickly can you get here for a no-hot-water emergency?
Usually within the hour for an active emergency call, depending on current call volume across the area. The dispatcher will give you a real estimate when you call, not a vague window.
Can a water heater repair wait until morning?
If it's just no hot water with no leaking or gas smell, yes, it can usually wait for a scheduled daytime visit at a lower cost than an after-hours dispatch. Any leaking, gas odor, or burning smell should be called in right away.
