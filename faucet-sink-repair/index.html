Faucet & Sink Repair in Spring, TX

Spring Express Plumbing repairs and installs kitchen and bathroom faucets and sinks throughout Spring, Texas. A dripping faucet is one of the most common calls we get, and it is also one of the least expensive to actually fix once we know which part has worn out.

Faucet drips almost always come down to a worn cartridge, O-ring, or valve seat, depending on the faucet style. Low flow from a single fixture, rather than the whole house, usually points to a clogged aerator or a failing cartridge restricting flow, not a house-wide pressure problem.

A faucet that drips constantly can waste a genuinely surprising amount of water over a month, and it's one of the cheapest fixes on this list relative to what ignoring it actually costs on the water bill.

2

What We Check

For a drip, we identify the faucet type first, compression, cartridge, ball, or ceramic disc, since each uses a different internal part and a different repair approach. Matching the wrong part to the wrong faucet style is a common reason a prior fix didn't hold.

For a sink replacement, we check the existing supply lines, shutoff valves, and drain configuration before installing, since an older sink sometimes has plumbing that needs updating to properly fit a current fixture.

We also check under-sink shutoff valves while we're already there. An old gate valve that no longer shuts off fully is a common discovery, and it's worth fixing at the same visit rather than finding out it doesn't work during an actual emergency later.

3

Repair or Replace

Cartridge or valve repair

The right call for a drip or low flow on a faucet that is otherwise in good shape, usually resolved in under an hour.

Full faucet replacement

Worth it when the faucet body itself is corroded, discontinued, or you simply want an upgrade while the plumbing is already accessible.

Sink replacement

Whether swapping a cracked sink or upgrading style, we handle the supply line and drain reconnection as part of the job.

Garbage disposal reconnection

If a sink swap involves an existing disposal unit, we reconnect or reinstall it as part of the same visit rather than leaving it disconnected under the cabinet.

Common Questions

Why does my faucet keep dripping after I already replaced a part?
Usually because the wrong part was matched to the faucet type, compression, cartridge, ball, or ceramic disc all use different internals. We identify the actual faucet type before repairing so the fix holds.
Can you match a new faucet to my existing sink?
In most cases yes, as long as the existing hole configuration matches or can reasonably be adapted. We check this before you buy a fixture that won't fit.
How long does a sink replacement take?
A standard sink swap with no supply line or drain issues takes one to two hours. If the existing plumbing needs updating to fit the new sink, we'll tell you that on site before starting.
Is a dripping faucet really worth fixing right away?
Yes. A steady drip adds up to real gallons wasted over a month, and it's usually a fast, inexpensive repair. Waiting rarely saves money and sometimes lets a worn valve seat damage the faucet body further.
