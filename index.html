Common Questions

Do you provide free estimates in Spring, TX?
Yes. Non-emergency estimates are free. If you call after hours for an active leak or a no-heat emergency, we still quote the repair before we start any work, but a true emergency dispatch carries an after-hours trip fee we tell you about upfront.
Is your 24/7 emergency line actually staffed overnight?
Yes. A real dispatcher answers day or night, not a voicemail box. If you call at 3 a.m. with a burst pipe, we can usually get a plumber to your door within the hour depending on current call volume.
Why do so many Spring homes get slab leaks?
Spring sits on the same expansive clay soil found across the Gulf Coast. That soil swells when wet and shrinks hard during a dry stretch, and the movement stresses the copper and cast iron lines running under an older slab foundation. Homes built before the mid-1990s are where we see slab leaks most often.
Do you cover the Montgomery County side of Spring, not just Harris County?
Yes. Spring straddles both counties, with the larger share in northern Harris County and a smaller section reaching into southern Montgomery County near the Spring Creek area. We dispatch to both sides under the same phone number and the same upfront pricing.
What should I do if a pipe freezes during a winter storm?
Texas freezes are rare but they happen, and a hard freeze like the one in February 2021 can split a pipe in an attic or exterior wall that was never built to handle it. Shut off your main water valve as soon as you notice no water pressure or a wet ceiling, then call us. We handle frozen and burst pipe repair as a standard call, not a specialty add-on.
