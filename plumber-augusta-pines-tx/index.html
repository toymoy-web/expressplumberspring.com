Augusta Pines at a Glance

Augusta Pines is a golf community in Spring, Texas 77389, in unincorporated Harris County. It is built around an 18-hole course and clubhouse on Augusta Pines Drive. Its sections include single-family homes, patio homes, and estate lots. Klein ISD serves the area, and Theiss Elementary is zoned to portions of it.

Some real estate sites list Augusta Pines under Tomball. Its mailing address is Spring 77389, and we treat it as part of the Spring area. Section boundaries vary, so we confirm your street by phone.

Patio Homes and Estate Lots Need Different Plumbing

A patio home and an estate home carry very different plumbing loads. A smaller home may be served well by one tank or a tankless unit. A large home with several bathrooms has longer supply runs and more fixtures, so hot water delivery and pressure matter more.

We size water heater and fixture work to the home, not to a template. Describe the house on the call, including bathrooms and any detached space, and we will talk through repair versus replacement. Our tankless page covers how tankless units work.

3

Water Service and Your Tax Bill

Water and sewer here have come through a municipal utility district. A 2015 Community Impact tax breakdown for the area listed Northwest Harris County MUD No. 19, but districts and rates can change, so treat your own bill as the authority.

If a bill looks high, the question is whether water is leaking or usage changed. A leak detection visit separates the two before you spend money on repairs.

Most Houston-area homes sit on concrete slabs, so a leak under the floor rarely shows itself. Warm spots on the floor, the sound of running water with everything off, and a rising bill are common signs. Locating the leak first avoids guesswork and unnecessary digging.

Tall Pines and Drain Lines

Augusta Pines takes its name from tall pines, and tree roots are a general cause of drain and sewer blockages anywhere trees stand near buried lines. We do not assume roots are your problem.

If drains keep backing up, we find the cause first, whether that is a fixture clog, a main line blockage, or something else. Our drain cleaning page explains how.

Common Questions

Is Augusta Pines in Spring or Tomball?
Its mailing address is Spring, TX 77389, though some real estate sites list it under Tomball. Read your street address to the dispatcher and we will confirm coverage.
Do you service patio homes as well as estate homes?
Yes. Describe the home when you call, including the number of bathrooms, so we can bring the right equipment.
Is a tankless water heater right for a large home?
It can be, depending on gas supply, venting, and how many fixtures run at once. We review those details before recommending it.
Which utility provides water in Augusta Pines?
A municipal utility district has provided water and sewer. Your own bill names the district, and it is the right contact for billing questions.
Do you work in the gated sections?
Yes. Tell the dispatcher your section and whether we need a gate code or should meet you at the entrance.
Do homes beside the golf course need anything special?
Not by default. If the problem is outdoors, such as an irrigation line or yard drain, tell the dispatcher when you call so we plan for it.
