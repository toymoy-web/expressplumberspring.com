Plumbing in One of Houston's Largest Suburbs

Spring Express Plumbing dispatches to Cypress, Texas around the clock. Cypress is an unincorporated community in Harris County, located entirely inside Houston's extraterritorial jurisdiction. It is big. The 2020 Census counted about 200,839 people across its two ZIP codes, 77429 and 77433.

Spring-Cypress Road ties our home base to this market. The road that passes Old Town Spring, at its Hardy Road intersection, carries on west to Cypress, where the post office sits on Spring Cypress Road. Cypress Creek runs through the area and separates Harris County Precincts 4 and 5.

A Market This Size Has No Single House Type

Cypress is served by Cy-Fair ISD, the third-largest school district in Texas, and by two volunteer fire departments, Cy-Fair VFD and Cypress Creek VFD. At this size no single housing type defines the area. Golf communities such as Blackhorse Ranch sit beside long-established neighborhoods, and Opendoor ranked 77433 the hottest ZIP code in the country for home buyers in 2023.

That mix means the first question on a Cypress call is about the house, not the street. A newer home and an older one fail in different ways, so we ask the year it was built, where the water heater sits, and where the main shutoff is before we dispatch.

What We Ask First on a Cypress Call

In many Texas homes the water heater sits in the attic, where a failure can drop water through the ceiling below. We ask whether yours is in the attic, the garage, or a closet, because that changes how fast we need to move and what to shut off first.

We also ask for the nearest major cross street, since Cypress is large enough that a neighborhood name alone does not tell us which road to take in. Cypress Creek runs through the area, and storms bring high water and power outages, so after heavy rain we check for broken lines and sump pumps without power.

Common Questions

Do you cover every ZIP code in Cypress?
Yes. We dispatch to both Cypress ZIP codes, 77429 and 77433, from the Spring-Cypress Road corridor near the Cypress post office out to the neighborhoods around Cypress Creek. Some addresses near Cypress carry a Houston mailing label, so call and we will confirm yours.
Do you answer emergency calls in Cypress overnight?
Yes. A real dispatcher answers at any hour and routes a plumber to Cypress. After-hours trips may carry a trip fee, which we state before any work begins.
Is Cypress a long trip from your Spring base?
It is farther than Klein or The Woodlands, roughly 15 to 20 miles depending on the neighborhood. We confirm the address and a realistic arrival window on the call instead of promising a number we cannot keep.
Can heavy rain around Cypress Creek cause plumbing problems?
It can. After heavy rain, watch for lines broken by shifting ground, sump pumps that lost power, and water heaters that got wet. We shut off water, repair what failed, and refer soaked walls and flooring to our water damage restoration team.
Do you install tankless water heaters in Cypress?
Yes. We size the unit to your household, confirm gas line and venting, and pull the permit when the project calls for one. The work follows the International Plumbing Code (IPC) as adopted in Texas. Annual descaling keeps a tankless unit running well in this area.
Do Cypress homes need a sump pump?
Most do not, since typical Houston-area homes sit on slabs without a basement. A pump helps with a crawlspace, a drainage pit, or a low area that collects water, and we look at the source of the water first.
What if my address says Houston but I think it is Cypress?
Call and read us the street address. Mailing labels do not always match the community name, and we route by the actual location.
