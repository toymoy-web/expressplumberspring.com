Emergency Plumbing in a City Built on Oil

Spring Express Plumbing dispatches to Humble, Texas, a separately incorporated city with its own water system since 1933. Humble started as a sawmill town along the Houston East & West Texas Railway in 1886, named for the town's first postmaster, Pleasant Smith Humble. Oil was discovered here in 1904, and by 1911 the Humble Oil Company, a direct predecessor of what's now ExxonMobil, was chartered right in this city.

That history matters for plumbing because Humble's original townsite carries genuinely old infrastructure, while the surrounding 77346 and 77396 ZIP codes, which also cover the Atascocita and Kingwood area under USPS naming, have grown with much newer construction.

By 1905, the Humble oilfield was the largest producing oil field in all of Texas, and the boomtown growth that followed left behind a real mix of early-1900s construction downtown and decades of subsequent development spreading outward. We treat that as useful context, not a guess, when a call comes in from a specific part of the city.
