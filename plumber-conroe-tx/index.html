A County Seat With Its Own Plumbing Story

Spring Express Plumbing dispatches to Conroe, Texas around the clock. Conroe is the county seat of Montgomery County, about 40 miles north of Houston, and its population passed 103,000 in 2023. The city incorporated in 1904 and has grown by annexation since 2007, from about 52.8 to 74.4 square miles.

Conroe is a separate market from Spring. It has its own city government, its own school district, Conroe ISD, and its own water story. Its groundwater supply was being pulled faster than it recharged, so the Lone Star Groundwater Conservation District ordered a 30 percent cut from 2009 levels by 2016. Since 2015 the San Jacinto River Authority has added surface water pumped from Lake Conroe.

Common Questions

Do you cover every ZIP code in Conroe?
Yes. We dispatch to Conroe ZIP codes 77301, 77302, 77303, and 77304, plus 77306. The southern codes 77384 and 77385 overlap Shenandoah and The Woodlands and are covered as well. Call with your street address if you are unsure which applies.
Is emergency service available in Conroe overnight?
Yes. A real dispatcher answers at any hour and routes a plumber to Conroe. After-hours trips may carry a trip fee, which we state before any work begins.
How far is Conroe from your Spring base?
About 18 to 20 miles up I-45, depending on where in Conroe you are. We confirm the address and a realistic arrival window on the call instead of promising a number we cannot keep.
Does Conroe's water supply change my plumbing needs?
It can. Conroe blends groundwater with surface water from Lake Conroe, and hardness and scale buildup vary by address. We check your fixtures and water heater before recommending a softener or filter.
Do you work on commercial buildings in Conroe?
Yes, light commercial work such as restroom fixtures, water heaters, and backflow devices. We schedule around business hours when we can.
Who handles permits for my Conroe project?
Inside Conroe city limits, permits usually go through the City of Conroe. Outside them, the county's rules apply. Either way the work follows the International Plumbing Code (IPC) as adopted in Texas, and we tell you which permit office applies once we have your address.
Is Conroe a separate trip or part of your normal area?
It is part of our normal dispatch area, not a special add-on. Conroe sits at the north end of the I-45 corridor we already cover through Shenandoah and The Woodlands.
