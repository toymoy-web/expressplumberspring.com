Burst Pipe Repair in Spring, TX

Spring Express Plumbing handles burst pipe emergencies across Spring, Texas around the clock. A burst pipe does not pause for business hours, and the longer water runs unchecked, the more it costs in water damage on top of the plumbing repair itself.

The single most useful thing anyone can do before we arrive is find and shut off the home's main water valve. That alone limits damage more than almost anything else, and our dispatcher will walk you through finding it if you're not sure where it is.

Burst pipes in Spring most often trace back to one of three causes. An aging galvanized or polybutylene line finally gives out, a hard freeze cracks a pipe in an attic or exterior wall, or a slab leak progresses to a full break rather than a slow seep.

2

What Happens When We Arrive

We confirm the main is off and locate the break, whether that means opening a wall cavity, checking an attic run, or tracing a line under a slab. Most accessible breaks, in a wall, attic, or crawlspace, are repaired the same visit.

If the burst is a symptom of a larger issue, an old galvanized system that's failed once and is likely to fail again elsewhere, we'll tell you that honestly rather than just patching the one spot and leaving the next failure to chance.

For breaks under a slab, we treat it as a slab leak repair and walk through the same reroute-versus-spot-repair decision covered on that page, since a burst slab line needs the same real trade-off conversation as a slow one.

Every repair meets the IPC as enforced by the Texas State Board of Plumbing Examiners, whether that's a section replacement, a fitting swap, or a larger repipe.

3

Repair Approaches

Section repair

Cutting out and replacing just the burst section, the fastest fix when the surrounding pipe is otherwise sound.

Fitting or coupling replacement

When the break is at a joint rather than mid-pipe, a new fitting often resolves it without a longer section replacement.

Broader repipe conversation

If this is the second or third burst on an aging system, we'll discuss whether continuing to patch individual failures still makes sense compared to a full repipe.

✓

What to Do Right Now, Before We Arrive

Shut off the main water valve. It's usually near the water meter or where the line enters the house.
Shut off electricity to any area where water is actively contacting outlets, switches, or fixtures, if you can do so safely.
Move furniture, electronics, and valuables away from the affected area.
Take photos of the damage for your insurance claim before you start any cleanup.
Do not attempt to repair the break yourself. A temporary patch can fail under pressure once the water is back on.

Common Questions

What's the first thing I should do if a pipe bursts?
Shut off the main water valve immediately, then call. Every minute the water runs adds to the damage, and shutting off the main is something you can do before a plumber even arrives.
How fast can you get to a burst pipe emergency?
Usually within the hour, depending on current call volume across the area. Tell the dispatcher it's an active burst and you'll be prioritized accordingly.
Will a burst pipe happen again after you repair it?
Not at that specific spot if repaired correctly. But if the underlying pipe material is old galvanized or polybutylene, another section can fail later, which is why we're upfront about that risk rather than only fixing what broke today.
