Windrose at a Glance

Windrose is a master-planned community of about 1,121 acres and roughly 2,200 homes, divided into ten sections. It sits south of FM 2920, west of Kuykendahl Road, and north of the Grand Parkway (SH 99), with Spring 77379 addresses. Friendswood Development began it in the late 1990s, and homes were built between 1998 and 2008.

Some sections are gated, including The Estates and Wyndham Trail. Klein ISD serves the community, and Benignus Elementary sits inside it. If you are unsure which section your street belongs to, give us the street name and we will place it.

Homes Now 18 to 28 Years Old

Homes finished between 1998 and 2008 are now roughly 18 to 28 years old. That is long enough for original fixtures, shutoff valves, and water heaters to be near or past typical service life. It does not mean anything is failing. We inspect first and tell you what is worn and what has years left.

Repiping is not a default answer for a home this age. It becomes worth discussing when we find repeated leaks or failing material. If that happens, our repiping page explains what the work involves.

3

Eagle Lake, the Golf Course, and Heavy Rain

The 20-acre Eagle Lake, an 18-hole golf course, trails, and two pools are the community's centerpieces. One real estate guide notes that Windrose has seen varying degrees of rainfall impact and advises buyers to check flood plain maps and past flood reports for a specific property.

From the plumbing side, a heavy storm means shutting off water if a line breaks, checking the water heater before it restarts, and having a drain or pump checked if water collects near the house. Soaked flooring and drywall are a separate restoration job.

4

Utility District and Community Association

Windrose is served by a municipal utility district for water and sewer, and a mandatory community association maintains common areas and enforces deed restrictions. Billing questions go to the district.

A pressure drop at the tap or a leak inside the house is something we can diagnose. If hard water is leaving scale on faucets, our softener page covers softener options.

Common Questions

Which ZIP code is Windrose in?
Windrose uses the Spring ZIP code 77379. Read your street address to the dispatcher so we can confirm coverage.
Do you work in the gated sections?
Yes. Tell the dispatcher your section and whether we need a gate code or should meet you at the entrance.
Which utility provides water in Windrose?
A municipal utility district provides water and sewer service. Your bill names the district, and it is the right contact for billing questions.
My Windrose home is about 20 years old. Should I repipe?
Not necessarily. Age alone is not a reason. Repiping is worth discussing when we find repeated leaks or failing material. We inspect first and explain the options.
Do you service homes on the lake or golf course?
Yes, with the same service and pricing. If the problem is outdoors, such as an irrigation line or yard drain, tell the dispatcher when you call.
Is Windrose in a flood zone?
It depends on the property. Check the flood plain map for your address. For plumbing, we handle shutoffs, broken lines, and water heater checks after a storm.
